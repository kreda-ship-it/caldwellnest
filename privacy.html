01 Who this covers

This Privacy Policy applies to CaldwellNest, a campus platform for verified Caldwell University students, operated by [OPERATOR NAME] doing business as Amahle Digital Creatives ("we," "us," "our"). CaldwellNest is not affiliated with Caldwell University.

02 What we collect

CategoryExamples
Account & identityYour @caldwell.edu email, chosen username, display name, and profile photo.

03 How we use it

  • To verify you're a current Caldwell student and give you access.
  • To operate core features: listings, marketplace, free items, events, saved items, and messaging.
  • To display your listings and public profile to other verified students.
  • To power NestBot responses (see section 07).
  • To keep the platform safe — moderating content, investigating abuse, and enforcing our Terms.
  • To understand how the platform is used and improve it (e.g., which categories are active).
  • To contact you about your account, security, or important service changes.

We do not sell your personal information.

04 Why we're allowed to process your data

We process your information to provide a service you've signed up for, to meet our legitimate interest in running a safe and functional platform, to comply with legal obligations, and — where applicable — with your consent (which you can withdraw). If you're in a region with specific legal-basis requirements, this section describes the grounds we rely on.

05 Who we share it with

  • Other users: your username, display name, profile photo, listings, and any messages you send are visible to the users you share them with. Anything you post publicly on the platform is visible to other verified students.
  • Service providers (subprocessors): companies that help us run CaldwellNest, listed in section 06. They process data on our behalf under their own terms.
  • Legal & safety: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or us.
  • Business transfer: if CaldwellNest is transferred to another entity (for example, once Amahle Digital Creatives is formally registered), your information may transfer with it under this policy.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

07 NestBot & AI

When you use NestBot, the text you send is transmitted to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a response. Please don't share sensitive personal information (such as government IDs, financial details, or health information) with NestBot. NestBot output is AI-generated and may be inaccurate — treat it as a helper, not an authority.

08 How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to operate the platform. Because CaldwellNest uses "soft" states, listings you mark sold, withdrawn, or expired remain in our database (they stop being visible to others) so we can support history, analytics, and moderation. Our activity log is append-only and retained for administrative and safety purposes. When you delete your account, we remove or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we must keep it for legal, security, or backup reasons.

09 Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction: you can view and update most of your profile and listings in the app.
  • Deletion: you can delete listings and request account deletion.
  • Requests: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, or to object to certain processing. Email us to make a request and we'll respond as required by applicable law.

To exercise any of these, contact amahledigitalcreatives@gmail.com.

10 Security

We use reasonable measures — including our providers' authentication and access controls — to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, and as a beta product CaldwellNest may have vulnerabilities. Please use a strong, unique password and let us know right away if you notice anything suspicious.

11 Age requirement

CaldwellNest is intended only for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we've collected data from someone under 18, we'll delete it. If you believe a minor is using CaldwellNest, contact us.

12 International note

CaldwellNest is operated for a New Jersey university community, and our providers may process and store data in the United States and other countries. By using CaldwellNest, you understand your information may be processed in the U.S. and elsewhere, where data protection laws may differ from those in your home country.

13 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CaldwellNest develops. We'll change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, may notify you in the app. Continuing to use CaldwellNest after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
