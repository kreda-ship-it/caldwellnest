01 Who this covers

This Privacy Policy applies to Nestrel, a campus platform for verified Caldwell University students, operated by [OPERATOR NAME] doing business as Amahle Digital Creatives ("we," "us," "our"). Nestrel is independent: it is not owned, operated, or endorsed by Caldwell University.

The standard we hold ourselves to

FERPA (the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act) is the federal law that protects students' education records — things like grades, transcripts, and class schedules kept by a school. It doesn't legally apply to Nestrel: we aren't a school, and we don't receive any records from Caldwell University. What we hold is information people give us directly through Nestrel.

We've chosen to build our data handling to FERPA's standards anyway. You sign up with your university email, and Nestrel holds your messages and campus activity — information that deserves that level of care whatever the law technically requires. In practice, that means:

  • We use your information only for the purposes we list (section 03), and nothing broader.
  • We don't sell it, advertise with it, or pass it on beyond the people and services named in sections 05 and 06.
  • We collect only what we need to verify you and run the platform (section 02).
  • You can see, correct, and delete your information, with a set response time (section 09).
  • We keep information for stated periods (section 08) and protect it with real safeguards (section 10).

If a university ever shares its own student records with us, we'll accept them only under a written agreement that gives the university control over how those records are used, kept, and destroyed — and we'll update this policy before that happens.

03 How we use it

  • To verify you're a current Caldwell student and give you access.
  • To operate core features: listings, marketplace, textbooks, free items, clubs and organizations, events, saved items, and messaging.
  • To display your listings and public profile to other verified students.
  • To power NestBot responses (see section 07).
  • To let club officers run their events — RSVP lists, door check-in, and feedback summaries (see section 05).
  • To keep the platform safe — moderating content, investigating abuse, and enforcing our Terms.
  • To understand how the platform is used and improve it, using totals and counts (e.g., which categories are active) rather than tracking what individual students do.
  • To contact you about your account, security, or important service changes.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as responding to a valid court order (see section 05).

Only these purposes

These are the only things we use your information for. If we ever want to use it for something new, we'll update this policy and tell you in the app before the change takes effect (see section 13).

We do not:

  • sell or rent your personal information;
  • show you targeted advertising, or let advertisers use your information;
  • share your information with data brokers or marketers;
  • use your listings, messages, or activity to train AI models.

05 Who we share it with

Here is who can see what.

  • Other students: your public profile (name, username, display name, photo, and — if you add them — bio, pronouns, major, and class year), whether your account is currently suspended (never the reason), your listings, and posts you publish for a club. Messages are visible to the person you send them to. When you post a listing, the email on your account is stored with it and can be accessed by other signed-in students. Poll results are shown to students as totals.
  • Club officers: officers who manage an event or run its check-in can see the name and email of everyone who RSVPs to that event, and whether they checked in. Officers who manage a club's membership can see its member list. Officers with analytics access can see how individual members voted in that club's polls. Officers can't see who follows a club. Event ratings and comments are shared anonymously with the organizers: they see the comments and an average rating, never names, and the average stays hidden until at least five people have responded — though in a small event, what a comment says may still hint at who wrote it. Officers must use what they see only for their club's own activities (see our Terms, section 04).
  • Nestrel administrators: the people who run Nestrel can access account information, listings, messages, reports, and activity records in order to verify students, review reports, investigate abuse, and keep the platform working. Administrators can currently open any conversation, not only reported ones. Admin access is limited to accounts we've given an admin role, and every data export is recorded in our activity log.
  • Caldwell University: Nestrel is independent of the university, and we do not share information that identifies individual students with it. If we share anything with the university — for example, as part of a pilot with a university office — it will be totals, such as how many students attended an event, and never figures for a group of fewer than five people. If that ever changes for a particular event or program, we'll tell you there, before you sign up — not after.
  • Service providers (subprocessors): companies that help us run Nestrel, listed in section 06.
  • Legal & safety: we may disclose information if required by law (for example, a valid subpoena or court order), or where needed to protect the safety of our users or the public, or to protect Nestrel against fraud or abuse. We share only what the situation requires.
  • Business transfer: if Nestrel is transferred to another entity (for example, once Amahle Digital Creatives is formally registered), your information may transfer with it under this policy.

No passing it on

Beyond the people and services listed above, we don't share your personal information with anyone. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. When we share totals or statistics instead of personal information, we remove names and anything else that could identify you, and we don't report on groups of fewer than five people.

A record of disclosures

If we ever disclose your personal information to someone outside the categories above — for example, in response to a legal request — we'll record who received it, what was shared, when, and why. You can ask to see that record for your account (section 09), unless the law prevents us from telling you.

09 Your rights & choices

These rights apply to every Nestrel user, wherever you live.

  • See your information: ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you. We'll send it within 45 days — the same window FERPA gives students — and usually much sooner.
  • Correct it: you can edit most of your profile and listings yourself in the app. For anything you can't edit, ask us. If we decide not to make a change, we'll explain why in writing.
  • Delete it: you can delete listings in the app. To delete your whole account, email us and we'll complete it within 30 days of confirming your request (section 08 explains what is kept, and for how long).
  • See our disclosure record: ask whether we've disclosed your information to anyone outside the categories in section 05, and to whom.
  • Raise a concern: if you disagree with how we use your information, tell us. We'll review it and reply in writing.

How to make a request. Email amahledigitalcreatives@gmail.com from your @caldwell.edu address, so we can confirm it's really you. If you no longer have access to that address, tell us and we'll find another way to confirm who you are. Requests are free, and using these rights won't affect your account.

10 Security

We protect your information with measures including:

  • Encryption: data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider, Supabase.
  • Access rules enforced by the database: each account can read only the records it's allowed to see, and those rules are enforced by the database itself — not just by the app's screens.
  • Limited extra access: only accounts we've given an admin role get administrator access. Club officer permissions (managing events, checking people in, viewing analytics) are granted one at a time.
  • Verified accounts: the database only accepts sign-ups from approved university email domains, and every email address must be confirmed.
  • Logging: moderation decisions and data exports are recorded in an activity log.

No system is perfectly secure, and as a beta product Nestrel may have vulnerabilities. Please use a strong, unique password and let us know right away if you notice anything suspicious.

If there's a breach. If we learn that someone has gained unauthorized access to your personal information, we'll notify affected users without unreasonable delay — and at least as quickly as New Jersey law requires — and tell you what happened, what information was involved, and what we're doing about it.
